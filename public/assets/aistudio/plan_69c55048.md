# Village Community Fund Management App --- Project Plan

## 1. Project Overview

Build a secure, low-cost mobile application for an approved village
community to manage monthly contributions and provide financial
assistance to people in need.

The app will support **Android and iOS** and provide **Tamil and
English** user interfaces. It will help the committee maintain
transparent records of money collected, assistance approved, and payouts
made.

### Primary objectives

1.  Collect monthly contributions from members. Contributions may be
    fixed or variable.
2.  Record full or partial payments and maintain payment history.
3.  Receive and review assistance requests for:
    -   Health and medical needs
    -   Financial hardship
    -   Education
    -   Other approved needs
4.  Approve and distribute funds to eligible beneficiaries.
5.  Track all money received and paid out.
6.  Show the current available fund, monthly collections, payouts, and
    pending requests.
7.  Provide reports and exportable records for committee review.

### Initial scope assumptions

-   One village/community is supported in the first release.
-   Only invited and approved members can sign in.
-   Beneficiaries do not need an app account.
-   Access is controlled by committee roles and permissions.
-   The app is a record-management and coordination system. Actual bank
    transfers or payment-gateway integration are not part of the first
    release unless separately approved.
-   Tamil and English are selectable. Store data independently of the
    display language so that names and records remain usable in either
    language.

## 2. Users and Roles

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Role                                Main responsibilities
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  President / Chairperson             View overall fund position, review
                                      governance reports, approve
                                      high-value requests

  Secretary                           Manage members, record requests,
                                      coordinate reviews, maintain
                                      records

  Treasurer                           Record and verify collections,
                                      execute approved payouts, reconcile
                                      accounts

  Committee Member                    Review requests and vote/approve
                                      according to assigned permissions

  Member / Contributor                View own contribution dues, payment
                                      history, and community-level
                                      summaries permitted by policy

  Auditor / Read-only                 View permitted financial records,
                                      reports, and audit history without
                                      changing data
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

Permissions should be configurable. Do not rely only on hiding buttons
in the mobile app; enforce every permission in the database.

## 3. Recommended Technology

### Mobile application

-   React Native
-   Expo
-   TypeScript
-   React Navigation
-   React Native Paper (or another consistent component library)
-   TanStack Query for server state and caching
-   Zustand for small pieces of client-side state
-   `i18next`, `react-i18next`, and `expo-localization` for
    Tamil/English localization

### Backend and data

-   Supabase Auth for authentication
-   Supabase PostgreSQL for application data
-   Row Level Security (RLS) for authorization
-   PostgreSQL functions / RPC for sensitive, transactional operations
-   Supabase Storage with private buckets for assistance documents
-   Supabase Edge Functions only where server-side integration or
    scheduled processing is needed

### Supporting services

-   Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications
-   GitHub for source control and CI
-   Expo Application Services (EAS) for builds and app distribution

### Architecture decision

Start with a **modular mobile app backed by Supabase**, not
microservices or Kubernetes. Keep business logic organized by feature
and place financial operations in secure database functions. A separate
Spring Boot service can be introduced later if the project develops
requirements that justify it.

## 4. Functional Modules

### 4.1 Authentication and membership

-   Invitation-only onboarding; no public self-registration.
-   Sign in and sign out.
-   Activate, suspend, and deactivate memberships.
-   Maintain member name, contact details, status, and membership start
    date.
-   Assign one or more roles to a member.
-   Allow an authorized administrator to invite a member.
-   Securely associate the authenticated account with an approved
    membership.

### 4.2 Contributions and collections

-   Configure monthly contribution plans.
-   Support fixed and variable contribution amounts.
-   Generate monthly dues for active members.
-   Record full and partial payments.
-   Record payment date, amount, method, reference, and who
    entered/verified it.
-   Generate receipts or receipt references.
-   Show each member's payment history and outstanding dues.
-   Prevent duplicate payment recording.

### 4.3 Beneficiaries and assistance requests

-   Register a beneficiary and capture only information necessary to
    assess the request.
-   Create requests under health, financial hardship, education, or
    other categories.
-   Record requested amount, reason, priority, supporting notes, and
    optional documents.
-   Track request status: Draft, Submitted, Under Review, Approved,
    Rejected, Paid, or Closed.
-   Keep beneficiary documents private and restrict access by role.
-   Record review comments and approval decisions.

### 4.4 Approvals and payouts

-   Configure approval rules and thresholds.
-   Support one or more approval steps.
-   Record who approved or rejected a request and when.
-   Allow the treasurer to pay only an approved request.
-   Record payout amount, date, payment method, reference, recipient,
    and proof where appropriate.
-   Prevent duplicate payouts and payouts that exceed available funds.
-   Support cancellation or correction through controlled reversal
    entries, not deletion.

### 4.5 Fund ledger

Use an auditable, double-entry ledger as the financial source of truth.

-   Record every contribution and payout as a posted financial
    transaction.
-   Ensure total debits equal total credits for each transaction.
-   Use `NUMERIC(14,2)` (or an appropriately sized decimal type) for
    monetary values; never use floating-point values for money.
-   Do not edit or delete posted ledger entries.
-   Correct mistakes through reversal transactions and replacement
    entries.
-   Derive balances from ledger entries rather than maintaining an
    independently editable balance.
-   Distinguish available funds from funds committed to approved but
    unpaid requests.
-   Reconcile recorded transactions against bank/cash statements
    periodically.

### 4.6 Dashboard and reports

Dashboard indicators:

-   Available balance
-   Committed amount
-   Total collected this month
-   Total paid out this month
-   Active members
-   Members with outstanding contributions
-   Assistance requests awaiting review
-   Beneficiaries supported this month

Reports:

-   Monthly and yearly collections
-   Member-wise contribution history
-   Outstanding contribution dues
-   Category-wise assistance
-   Approved, rejected, pending, and paid requests
-   Monthly and yearly payout history
-   Fund movement and ledger statement
-   Audit activity report

Support CSV export initially. Add formatted Excel (`.xlsx`) export if
required. Apply the same authorization rules to reports and exports as
to on-screen data.

### 4.7 Notifications

-   Monthly contribution reminders.
-   Payment confirmation.
-   New assistance request notification to authorized reviewers.
-   Approval/rejection updates.
-   Payout confirmation.
-   Important committee announcements.

Do not include sensitive beneficiary details in push notification text.
Direct users to the authenticated app for protected information.

### 4.8 Tamil and English

-   Provide a language selector in the app.
-   Keep translation keys in English and maintain separate Tamil and
    English translation files.
-   Translate navigation, labels, validation messages, notifications,
    and report headings.
-   Store internal status codes and database field names in a stable
    language-independent form.
-   Support Unicode Tamil names and addresses.
-   Test Tamil font rendering, text wrapping, search, and exports on
    both Android and iOS.
-   Initial release: Tamil or English per user. A side-by-side bilingual
    display can be considered after the core workflows are stable.

## 5. High-Level Data Model

Use UUID primary keys and include `organization_id` on business records,
even though the first release serves one community. This keeps the
design extensible to additional villages later.

  Table / entity           Purpose
  ------------------------ -----------------------------------------
  `organizations`          Village/community details
  `profiles`               Authenticated user's profile
  `memberships`            Approved membership and status
  `roles`                  Available roles
  `permissions`            Individual permissions
  `role_permissions`       Permissions assigned to roles
  `member_roles`           Roles assigned to members
  `contribution_plans`     Fixed/variable contribution rules
  `contribution_dues`      Monthly dues generated per member
  `payments`               Contribution payment records
  `payment_receipts`       Receipt/reference details
  `beneficiaries`          People receiving assistance
  `assistance_requests`    Requests for support
  `assistance_documents`   Private supporting documents
  `approvals`              Review and approval decisions
  `payouts`                Actual assistance disbursements
  `fund_accounts`          Cash/bank fund accounts
  `fund_transactions`      Financial transaction headers
  `ledger_entries`         Debit/credit ledger lines
  `audit_logs`             Important user and financial actions
  `notifications`          Notification records and delivery state

Create database views or secure RPC functions for dashboard summaries
and reports. Avoid exposing unrestricted tables directly to the mobile
app.

## 6. Critical Workflows

### 6.1 Monthly contribution

1.  Authorized user configures or confirms the monthly contribution
    plan.
2.  System generates dues for active members.
3.  Members and/or the treasurer can view outstanding dues according to
    permissions.
4.  Treasurer records a payment and its method/reference.
5.  The system validates the member, amount, period, and duplicate
    reference.
6.  Payment record and balanced ledger entries are committed atomically.
7.  Receipt/reference is made available and the dashboard is refreshed.

### 6.2 Assistance request and payout

1.  Authorized user creates a request for a beneficiary.
2.  Secretary or reviewer validates the request and supporting
    information.
3.  Committee members approve or reject according to configured rules.
4.  The system checks that the approved amount is within available
    funds.
5.  Treasurer records the payout.
6.  Payout record, ledger entries, and audit record are committed
    atomically.
7.  The request is marked Paid and the dashboard is refreshed.

### 6.3 Member onboarding

1.  Authorized administrator creates or selects a membership record.
2.  Administrator sends an invitation.
3.  User completes authentication.
4.  Backend links the authenticated account to the approved membership.
5.  Role-based access is applied and audited.

## 7. Security and Financial Controls

These controls are release requirements, not optional enhancements.

-   Enable RLS on every table exposed through Supabase APIs.
-   Deny access by default and grant only the minimum required
    permissions.
-   Store role assignments in protected database tables; do not trust
    user-editable metadata for authorization.
-   Never include the Supabase service-role key or other server secrets
    in the mobile application.
-   Use the publishable client key with RLS.
-   Put sensitive financial actions in database functions with strict
    authorization checks.
-   Use database transactions and appropriate row locking to prevent
    concurrent payouts from spending the same funds.
-   Use idempotency keys or unique transaction references to prevent
    duplicate submissions.
-   Keep posted financial entries immutable; use reversals for
    corrections.
-   Store beneficiary documents in private storage buckets and use
    short-lived signed URLs.
-   Minimize collection of personal and medical information; restrict
    access and retention.
-   Use secure token storage provided by the platform (Keychain/Keystore
    through an appropriate Expo-compatible library).
-   Consider MFA for privileged administrators.
-   Audit role changes, approvals, payment verification, payouts,
    reversals, and exports.
-   Do not treat a payment as completed while offline. Financial actions
    require server confirmation.

Before production, review applicable Indian privacy, data-protection,
accounting, tax, and payment requirements with qualified local advisers.
The app should not be represented as a bank, payment processor, or
regulated financial service without the required assessment.

## 8. Offline and Reliability Strategy

-   Cache the dashboard and previously viewed, non-sensitive history
    where appropriate.
-   Allow drafts of assistance requests to be prepared offline if
    needed.
-   Clearly show when displayed data was last synchronized.
-   Queue non-financial draft changes for later synchronization.
-   Require a live server response before confirming a contribution or
    payout.
-   Handle retries safely using idempotency keys.
-   Provide clear error states for network failures and stale data.

## 9. Backup, Recovery, and Operations

Financial records require a recovery plan independent of the primary
hosting provider.

-   Configure automated database backups or scheduled encrypted database
    dumps to separate storage.
-   Keep backup credentials outside the application and source
    repository.
-   Suggested retention: daily backups for 30 days, weekly backups for 3
    months, and monthly backups for 12 months.
-   Keep a second copy in a separate account or storage location.
-   Perform and document a restore test at least monthly.
-   Validate restored contribution totals, payout totals, ledger
    balance, and key record counts.
-   Restrict production access and maintain an incident/contact
    procedure.

A free hosting tier may be suitable for development or a small pilot,
but it may have inactivity pauses, limited backup/recovery features,
quotas, and no production SLA. Confirm current provider limits before
launch. Do not use a free-tier deployment for real community funds until
independent backups, restore tests, and operational ownership are in
place.

## 10. Delivery Roadmap

The phases below are sequential milestones. Each phase should finish
with a demonstrable build and tested acceptance criteria.

### Phase 0 --- Requirements and governance

-   Confirm committee roles and decision-making rules.
-   Confirm contribution rules: fixed/variable, due dates, partial
    payments, arrears, and waivers.
-   Confirm assistance categories, eligibility, approval thresholds, and
    emergency process.
-   Decide whether the app records cash, bank, or both.
-   Define who can see member-level and beneficiary-level information.
-   Agree on receipt format, reports, and audit requirements.
-   Confirm language behavior and app name/branding.

**Exit criteria:** Approved requirements, permission matrix, workflow
diagrams, and initial screen list.

### Phase 1 --- Project foundation

-   Create GitHub repository and development workflow.
-   Set up Expo React Native TypeScript project.
-   Add navigation, theme, reusable components, and environment
    configuration.
-   Configure Supabase project and local development workflow.
-   Add database migration structure and seed data.
-   Set up Tamil/English localization.
-   Add linting, formatting, and basic unit tests.

**Exit criteria:** App launches on Android and iOS; language switching
works; development and staging configuration are documented.

### Phase 2 --- Authentication, members, and permissions

-   Implement invitation-only authentication.
-   Create profiles, memberships, roles, and permissions.
-   Build member list, member details, add/edit, and status management.
-   Implement RLS policies and permission checks.
-   Add audit events for membership and role changes.

**Exit criteria:** Only invited users can sign in; users can access only
the screens and records allowed by their roles; authorization is tested
at the database level.

### Phase 3 --- Contributions

-   Build contribution plan management.
-   Generate monthly dues.
-   Build member contribution history and outstanding dues.
-   Record full and partial payments.
-   Generate receipt references.
-   Add contribution notifications.
-   Add monthly collection reports.

**Exit criteria:** Test cases prove correct dues, partial payments,
duplicate prevention, receipts, and monthly totals.

### Phase 4 --- Ledger and fund controls

-   Implement fund accounts, transaction headers, and ledger entries.
-   Implement secure contribution-posting database function.
-   Implement balance and committed-fund calculations.
-   Add reversal workflow and audit history.
-   Add reconciliation report.

**Exit criteria:** Every posted transaction balances; concurrent and
duplicate requests are safely handled; available balance agrees with the
ledger.

### Phase 5 --- Beneficiaries, assistance, approvals, and payouts

-   Build beneficiary management.
-   Build assistance request creation and review.
-   Add private document storage.
-   Implement approval rules and approval history.
-   Implement secure payout function with balance and permission checks.
-   Add payout proof/reference and notifications.

**Exit criteria:** Only approved requests can be paid; duplicate or
over-budget payouts are rejected; all actions are auditable.

### Phase 6 --- Dashboard, reports, and exports

-   Implement dashboard summary RPC/view.
-   Add monthly and yearly reports.
-   Add member, category, and request-status reports.
-   Add CSV export and, if required, Excel export.
-   Verify report and export permissions.

**Exit criteria:** Dashboard totals reconcile with ledger data and
exports match the authorized report results.

### Phase 7 --- Quality, security, and release

-   Test on representative Android and iOS devices.
-   Test Tamil and English layouts and exports.
-   Add integration tests for financial database functions.
-   Test role access, RLS, duplicate requests, concurrent payouts, and
    reversals.
-   Test poor network behavior and recovery from failed requests.
-   Configure backups and complete a restore drill.
-   Prepare privacy notice, user guidance, support contacts, and release
    notes.
-   Distribute an internal pilot build and collect feedback before wider
    release.

**Exit criteria:** Security review completed, backup restore
demonstrated, financial reconciliation passed, and pilot users sign off.

## 13. Definition of Done

A feature is complete only when:

-   Its acceptance criteria are met.
-   UI works on Android and iOS.
-   Tamil and English strings are available.
-   Loading, empty, validation, and error states are handled.
-   Database authorization and RLS are tested.
-   Sensitive actions are audited.
-   Financial operations are atomic and idempotent where applicable.
-   Automated tests cover important business rules.
-   No server secrets are present in the mobile bundle.
-   Relevant documentation is updated.

## 14. Decisions to Confirm Before Implementation

1.  What is the official app/community name?
2.  Are contributions monthly only, or can members contribute at any
    time?
3.  Can members see only their own payments, or should they see
    aggregate member-level collection information?
4.  Who can approve assistance, and what approval thresholds apply?
5.  Will the treasurer record cash and bank payments, or bank payments
    only?
6.  Should the app integrate with UPI/payment gateways in a later phase,
    or remain a manual record system?
7.  Should the first release support one committee or multiple
    villages/organizations?
8.  Which reports must be available to all members versus committee
    members only?

## Recommended First Build

Keep the first usable release focused:

1.  Invitation-only login and member roles.
2.  Member management.
3.  Monthly contribution dues and payment recording.
4.  A reliable ledger and current fund balance.
5.  Assistance requests, approvals, and payout recording.
6.  Dashboard and basic monthly reports.
7.  Tamil/English support, audit history, and tested backups.

Avoid adding payment-gateway integration, complex offline financial
processing, multi-village administration, and advanced analytics until
the core financial workflow is proven and trusted.
